How does the Robux fee calculator work?

For a 30% fee, a sale of 1,000 Robux leaves approximately 700 Robux before any other applicable adjustments. To work backward, divide your desired earnings by the percentage you keep.

Actual Roblox revenue shares vary by product type, sale channel and applicable policies. The fee is adjustable so you can model the rate relevant to your situation. This tool is not affiliated with Roblox.
