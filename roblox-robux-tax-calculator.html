How the Roblox Robux Tax Calculator Works

The calculator estimates the portion of a Robux transaction retained after a percentage-based fee. The default 30% is an example commonly associated with some Roblox transactions, but it is not a universal fee for every sale or earning method.

Calculate Robux After a Fee

Estimated Robux received = Sale Price × (1 − Fee Rate).

For a 100 Robux sale and a 30% fee, the mathematical result is 70 Robux received and 30 Robux deducted.

Reverse Robux Calculation

Required Price = Target Robux ÷ (1 − Fee Rate).

For a target of 100 Robux at a 30% fee, the mathematical required price is approximately 142.86 Robux. Since Robux prices generally use whole numbers, the calculator rounds the required listing price upward.

Important Fee Differences

Actual Roblox revenue shares depend on the product, experience, creator arrangements, transaction pathway and current platform rules. Use the correct fee for your specific transaction rather than assuming every Robux purchase has the same deduction.

Robux Tax FAQ

How much is 100 Robux after a 30% fee?

The mathematical result is 70 Robux.

How much should I charge to receive 100 Robux?

At an assumed 30% fee, approximately 143 Robux is required before accounting for platform-specific payout rounding.

Does Roblox always charge 30%?

No. Fees and revenue shares vary by transaction type and Roblox policy. Verify the applicable rate.

Is this connected to my Roblox account?

No. This is an independent estimation calculator.
