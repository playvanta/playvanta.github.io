How to Plan a CS2 Buy Round

Counter-Strike 2's economy rewards careful spending and coordination. Choosing a weapon, armor and utility without exceeding your team's budget is an important part of competitive play.

Full Buy

A full buy typically includes a strong primary weapon, appropriate armor and useful grenades. On CT side, a defuse kit may also be valuable.

Force Buy

A force buy spends limited resources to contest a round. It can involve lower-cost weapons and reduced utility.

Eco Round

An eco round limits spending to preserve money for a future purchase. Teams may choose pistols or save most of their money.

Why Utility Matters

Smoke grenades, flashbangs, HE grenades and incendiaries or Molotovs can create tactical opportunities. A strong weapon without supporting utility is not always the best purchase.

CS2 Economy FAQ

What is a full buy in CS2?

A full buy generally means purchasing a competitive weapon, armor and useful utility while considering team economy.

Does this calculator track my CS2 inventory?

No. It is a manual buy-round planning tool.

Are weapon prices always current?

Not necessarily. Game updates can change prices or availability. Confirm prices in the current CS2 buy menu.

Can I use it for both T and CT?

Yes. The weapon list and fire grenade price change according to the selected side.
