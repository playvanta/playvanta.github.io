Understanding MMR

Matchmaking Rating is used to estimate player skill in competitive playlists. Winning generally raises MMR and losing generally lowers it. Consistent performance over many matches matters more than a single result.

About Rocket League

Drive. Fly. Score.

Rocket League combines high-speed cars and soccer in competitive matches. Players develop aerial control, positioning, rotations and teamwork to climb the ranks.
