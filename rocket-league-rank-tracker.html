How Rocket League MMR Works

Matchmaking Rating, or MMR, is a numerical rating used by Rocket League's matchmaking system. Visible competitive ranks relate to rating, but thresholds can vary by playlist and season.

MMR Needed for Your Goal

MMR Remaining = Target MMR − Current MMR.

Wins Needed Without Losses

Divide the remaining MMR by your assumed gain per win and round up. This gives a simple best-case estimate if you win every match.

Estimating Progress with a Win Rate

Expected MMR per Match = (Win Rate × MMR per Win) − (Loss Rate × MMR per Loss).

If the result is positive, the calculator estimates the number of matches required. If it is zero or negative, your assumptions do not produce an upward trend.

Important Limitations

This calculator is not an official rank lookup. It does not retrieve your Epic Games account, verify live rank thresholds or predict matchmaking outcomes.

Rocket League Rank Tracker FAQ

Does this show my live Rocket League rank?

No. You manually enter your MMR and goal.

How much MMR do I gain per win?

It varies. The default value is an editable assumption, not a guaranteed gain.

Are rank thresholds the same every season?

Not necessarily. Rank distributions and thresholds can change by season and playlist.

Can I use this for 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3?

Yes. Enter the appropriate rating and target for the selected playlist.
