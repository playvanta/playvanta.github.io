How the tracker works

Enter your current and target MMR. The calculator estimates the number of consecutive wins needed and the number of games required at your expected win rate, using average MMR gained and lost.

MMR changes and rank boundaries depend on playlist, season and matchmaking conditions. This calculator does not automatically determine your current rank or retrieve live player data.
