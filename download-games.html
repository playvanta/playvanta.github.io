Help & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Does PlayVanta provide the game files?

No. PlayVanta links to official publisher websites and authorized storefronts. We do not host game installers.

Are all games free?

No. Some games are paid, while others are free-to-play and may offer optional purchases. Prices and availability can vary by platform and region.

Can I find tools for these games?

Yes. Games with existing PlayVanta calculators have an additional tool button. More tools can be added as the website grows.

Why are some images different from the game artwork?

Some external image providers may change or block their image URLs. Game cards display a branded fallback if their artwork cannot be loaded.
