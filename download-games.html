Frequently Asked Questions

About Downloading Games

Does PlayVanta host game downloads?

No. PlayVanta is a game discovery directory. Download links lead to official game, publisher or platform websites.

Are all the games free?

No. Games are labeled Free-to-Play or Paid. Pricing and availability can vary by region, platform and edition.

Are the download links safe?

We aim to link only to official websites and authorized storefronts. Always verify the destination and follow the publisher's installation instructions.

Will more games be added?

Yes. The directory is designed to expand as PlayVanta adds more games, calculators and guides.
