How Nether Travel Works

In Minecraft, traveling one block horizontally in the Nether corresponds to eight blocks in the Overworld.

To convert Overworld coordinates to Nether coordinates, divide X and Z by 8. To convert Nether coordinates to Overworld coordinates, multiply X and Z by 8.

The Y coordinate is not scaled. Actual portal linking also depends on nearby portals and valid portal locations, so these are target coordinates rather than a guarantee of where a portal will appear.
