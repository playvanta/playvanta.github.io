How Minecraft Nether Coordinates Work

In Minecraft, traveling one block horizontally in the Nether corresponds to eight blocks in the Overworld. This makes Nether travel useful for connecting distant bases, villages and farms.

Overworld → Nether: X ÷ 8, Z ÷ 8
Nether → Overworld: X × 8, Z × 8

Example: Overworld to Nether

If your Overworld base is at X = 800 and Z = -400, the corresponding Nether coordinates are X = 100 and Z = -50.

How to Link Nether Portals

  • Record your portal's Overworld X and Z coordinates.
  • Divide both values by eight.
  • Travel to the calculated location in the Nether.
  • Build a safe portal at an appropriate height and test the connection.
  • If it links incorrectly, inspect nearby portals and adjust placement.

Does the Y Coordinate Matter?

Y does not scale by eight. However, vertical distance can affect which nearby portal is selected. The calculator therefore leaves Y unchanged as a reference rather than claiming it predicts the ideal portal height.

Minecraft Nether Calculator FAQ

What is the Nether coordinate ratio?

The horizontal coordinate ratio is 8:1. One Nether block corresponds to eight Overworld blocks.

Do negative coordinates work?

Yes. Negative X and Z values follow the same division and multiplication rules.

Does this work in Java and Bedrock Edition?

The 8:1 horizontal coordinate relationship applies to both, although portal-search and linking behavior can differ.

Why does my portal connect to the wrong location?

Nearby existing portals and the game's portal-selection rules can affect the destination. Coordinate conversion alone does not guarantee a specific link.

Should I round decimal coordinates?

Block placement requires integer coordinates. You can round to a nearby block, then test the resulting portal connection.
