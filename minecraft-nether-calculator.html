🧱 Minecraft Nether Calculator

Find the corresponding X and Z coordinates for a portal in Minecraft's Overworld or Nether. The horizontal scale ratio is 8:1.

Portal coordinate converter

Target dimension coordinates
—

Y (height) is not scaled by 8. Portal placement and linking also depend on nearby existing portals and valid placement locations; this tool gives target horizontal coordinates, not a guaranteed portal link.

Example

Overworld X = 800 and Z = −400 becomes Nether X = 100 and Z = −50. Reverse the direction to multiply Nether X and Z by 8.
