How Nether coordinates work

In Minecraft, horizontal travel in the Nether corresponds to eight times the distance in the Overworld. Divide Overworld X and Z by 8 to estimate Nether coordinates, or multiply Nether X and Z by 8 to estimate Overworld coordinates.

The Y coordinate is not scaled. Actual portal linking also depends on nearby portals and the game's portal-search rules.
