How Minecraft Nether Coordinates Work

One block traveled horizontally in the Nether corresponds to eight blocks in the Overworld. Enter your X and Z coordinates and choose a conversion direction to calculate the corresponding location.

For example, Overworld X 800 and Z 400 convert to Nether X 100 and Z 50. Portal linking can still be affected by nearby portals and world geometry.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Nether coordinate ratio?

The Overworld-to-Nether horizontal coordinate ratio is 8:1.

Does the Y coordinate change?

Y is not multiplied or divided by eight. The actual portal placement height may differ.
