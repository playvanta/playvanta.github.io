This is a simplified planning tool for limited character wishes. It does not simulate drop rates, guarantee rules, Capturing Radiance, Fate Points or other banner-specific mechanics. A 180-pull budget is a conservative planning assumption, not a universal requirement.

How the Genshin Wish Calculator Works

One wish costs 160 Primogems when purchased with Primogems. The calculator divides your Primogems by 160, rounds down to whole wishes and adds your Intertwined Fates.

Primogems to Wishes Formula

Wishes = floor(Primogems ÷ 160) + Intertwined Fates.

Understanding Character-Banner Pity

The standard limited character wish system has a 90-wish ceiling for obtaining a 5-star character. Actual outcomes can occur earlier, and the featured-character guarantee depends on banner rules and your history.

Planning a Conservative Budget

Some players plan around two 90-wish cycles as a conservative budget. This tool lets you compare your saved wishes against that amount, while acknowledging that newer or special banner mechanics can affect outcomes.

Example

16,000 Primogems equal 100 wishes. Add 10 Intertwined Fates for 110 total wishes, with zero Primogems left over.

Genshin Wish FAQ

How many Primogems is one wish?

One wish costs 160 Primogems.

How many wishes is 16,000 Primogems?

16,000 Primogems converts to 100 wishes.

Does pity carry over?

Pity generally carries over between successive banners of the same applicable type, subject to the game's current rules. Different banner categories may track pity separately.

Does this predict my exact pull result?

No. It estimates your wish budget and does not predict random outcomes.
