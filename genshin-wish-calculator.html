How the wish calculator works

One wish costs 160 Primogems. This calculator combines the wishes purchasable with your Primogems and your existing Fates, then compares the total with the selected banner's standard hard-pity threshold.

The pity calculation is a simplified estimate. Banner-specific guarantees, featured-item rules, pity carryover and other mechanics are not modeled. Use the appropriate Fate type for your chosen banner.
