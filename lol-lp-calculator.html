How the LP calculator works

For a simple LP goal, subtract your current LP from your target. The calculator divides the difference by your expected LP gain to estimate consecutive wins. It also estimates progress using your win rate and average LP lost in defeats.

This is a simplified LP model. Rank promotions, demotions, hidden MMR and Riot's current ranked rules can change actual results. For goals across divisions, enter a cumulative LP target rather than assuming the game treats all divisions as one continuous LP counter.
