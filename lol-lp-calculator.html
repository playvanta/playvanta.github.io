Actual LP changes can vary because of MMR, rank transitions, promotions and demotions.

How to Calculate League of Legends LP Gains

Enter your current LP, expected win rate, LP gained per win, LP lost per defeat and number of matches. The calculator estimates the average LP change across those matches.

This is a statistical projection rather than a guarantee. Your actual rank progression will depend on match outcomes and the ranked system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I estimate LP gains from win rate?

Multiply the win rate by LP gained per win, subtract the loss rate multiplied by LP lost per defeat, then multiply by the number of matches.

Does the calculator predict promotions and demotions?

No. It calculates an estimated LP change before accounting for tier boundaries and ranked-system adjustments.
