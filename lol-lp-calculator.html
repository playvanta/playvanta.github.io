How to Calculate League of Legends LP Progress

League Points, or LP, are used to track ranked progress in League of Legends. Winning ranked games can award LP, while losing games can reduce it. The exact amounts depend on matchmaking and ranked-system rules.

Calculate Expected LP per Game

Expected LP per Game = (Win Rate × LP per Win) − (Loss Rate × LP per Loss).

For example, at a 55% win rate, +25 LP per win and −23 LP per loss, the expected gain is 3.4 LP per game.

Calculate Games to Your Goal

Divide the additional LP needed by your expected net LP per game and round up. If your expected net LP is zero or negative, the assumptions do not predict consistent upward progress.

Why LP Gains and Losses Change

LP changes may be influenced by your matchmaking rating relative to your visible rank and by current ranked rules. The calculator treats your entered gains and losses as fixed averages.

Important Difference Between LP and Rank

This tool calculates a requested amount of additional LP. It does not automatically translate that amount into a particular rank or division. Rank boundaries and promotion behavior can affect real progression.

League of Legends LP Calculator FAQ

What win rate do I need to climb?

Under fixed LP assumptions, your win rate must be high enough that expected LP gained from wins exceeds expected LP lost from defeats.

Why do I gain less LP than I lose?

Your visible rank and underlying matchmaking rating may be misaligned, among other ranked-system factors.

Does this track my Riot account?

No. All values are entered manually.

Can this predict my exact future rank?

No. It provides a simplified statistical projection based on your assumptions, not a prediction of match outcomes.
