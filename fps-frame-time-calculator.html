What Is Frame Time in Gaming?

Frame time measures how long a frame takes to render, usually in milliseconds. FPS tells you how many frames are produced per second, while frame time expresses the duration of each frame.

Frame Time (ms) = 1000 ÷ FPS

FPS = 1000 ÷ Frame Time (ms)

What Is 1% Low FPS?

1% low FPS is a performance statistic intended to describe slower frames. It is typically derived from the slowest portion of a frame-time sample, but exact calculation methods vary between benchmarking tools. A large difference between average FPS and 1% low FPS may indicate inconsistent performance.

Does More FPS Always Mean Smoother Gaming?

Higher FPS reduces the average time between frames, but smoothness also depends on frame-time consistency, synchronization, display behavior and input latency. Average FPS alone cannot fully describe stuttering.

FPS Calculator FAQ

How many milliseconds is 144 FPS?

144 FPS corresponds to approximately 6.94 milliseconds per frame.

How many milliseconds is 240 FPS?

240 FPS corresponds to approximately 4.17 milliseconds per frame.

Is frame time the same as input lag?

No. Frame time is one component of the rendering process. Total input-to-display latency also includes input processing, buffering, display processing and other delays.

Can this tool measure my PC's FPS?

No. Enter measurements from your game or benchmarking software. This calculator converts and explains the numbers; it does not benchmark your computer.
