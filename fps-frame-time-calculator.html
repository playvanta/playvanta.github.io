Frame time in milliseconds is calculated as 1000 ÷ FPS. These figures represent average or equivalent frame durations; they do not measure actual frame pacing, input latency or stuttering. A 1% low FPS value is a benchmark statistic rather than an individual frame-time reading.

FPS vs Frame Time

FPS measures how many frames are displayed per second. Frame time measures the duration of a frame in milliseconds. Lower and more consistent frame times generally contribute to smoother motion.
