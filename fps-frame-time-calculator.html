Average Frame Time—

⚡ Performance Guide

Frame time in milliseconds = 1000 ÷ FPS.

This calculates an average frame interval, not real frame pacing, input lag or stutters.

What Is Frame Time in Gaming?

Frame time is the amount of time represented by one frame at a given frame rate. At 60 FPS, the average frame interval is about 16.67 milliseconds. At 144 FPS, it is approximately 6.94 milliseconds.

Monitor refresh interval uses the same calculation with Hz instead of FPS. A 144 Hz display has a refresh interval of approximately 6.94 milliseconds.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate frame time?

Divide 1000 by your FPS to get the average milliseconds per frame.

Does lower frame time mean smoother gameplay?

Lower average frame time generally corresponds to higher FPS, but consistent frame pacing is also important for smooth gameplay.
