What Is Frame Time?

Frame time is the amount of time each frame takes to render, measured in milliseconds. Lower frame times generally mean a higher frame rate.

The formula is: Frame Time = 1000 / FPS

Why Does FPS Matter?

Higher FPS can make games appear smoother and reduce the time between newly rendered frames. This is especially useful in competitive games such as Valorant, Counter-Strike 2 and Apex Legends.

Actual smoothness also depends on frame-time consistency, display refresh rate, input latency and other factors.
