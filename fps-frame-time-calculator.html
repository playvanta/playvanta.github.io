Understanding FPS and Frame Time

Frame time is the duration of one rendered frame. At 60 FPS, each frame takes about 16.67 milliseconds; at 120 FPS, about 8.33 milliseconds.

This calculator shows ideal average frame intervals. Actual input latency and perceived smoothness also depend on frame pacing, hardware, display refresh rate and other factors.
