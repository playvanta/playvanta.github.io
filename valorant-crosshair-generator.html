Crosshair Guide

How to Choose the Best Valorant Crosshair

There is no single best Valorant crosshair for every player. Different players prefer different sizes, colors and line styles based on their visibility preferences, screen resolution and aiming habits.

1. Small Crosshairs for Precision

Compact crosshairs take up less screen space. They can be useful for players who prefer a minimal reticle while holding angles or aiming at distant opponents.

2. Larger Crosshairs for Visibility

A slightly larger crosshair can be easier to locate during fast movement and hectic fights. The tradeoff is that it occupies more of the area around your target.

3. Center Dot Crosshairs

Center dot crosshairs provide a small focal point in the middle of your screen. Some players enjoy the minimal appearance, while others prefer visible lines for easier tracking.

4. Crosshair Colors

Cyan, green, white and yellow are common choices because they can stand out against different map environments. The best option is whichever remains visible to you during actual gameplay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Valorant Crosshair FAQ

How do I import a crosshair code into Valorant?

Open Valorant and navigate to Settings → Crosshair. Find the Import Profile Code option, paste your crosshair code and confirm the import.

What is the best crosshair color in Valorant?

There is no universal best color. Cyan, green, white and yellow are popular options. Choose a color that stays visible against different map backgrounds.

Should I use a center dot crosshair?

A center dot can provide a minimal aiming reference, but it may be harder for some players to see. Experiment with both center dot and line-based crosshairs to find your preference.

Can a crosshair improve my aim?

A comfortable and visible crosshair can help you maintain visual focus, but aiming skill also depends on practice, positioning, crosshair placement and mouse control.
