Crosshair Guide

How to Choose the Best Valorant Crosshair

There is no single best crosshair for every Valorant player. Your ideal crosshair depends on personal preference, visibility and aiming habits.

1. Small Crosshairs for Precision

Compact crosshairs occupy less screen space and may help players who prefer a minimal reticle while holding angles or aiming at distant targets.

2. Larger Crosshairs for Visibility

Larger crosshairs are often easier to locate during fast movement and intense fights. However, they can cover more of your target.

3. Center Dot Crosshairs

Center dot crosshairs provide a minimal aiming reference. Some players prefer this style, while others find traditional crosshair lines easier to see.

4. Choosing Your Crosshair Color

Cyan, green, white and yellow are common crosshair colors. Choose a color that stands out clearly against the environments you encounter in-game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Valorant Crosshair FAQ

How do I import a crosshair code into Valorant?

Open Valorant and navigate to Settings → Crosshair. Select the Import Profile Code option, paste your code and confirm.

What is the best crosshair color in Valorant?

There is no universally best color. Cyan, green, white and yellow are popular choices because they can remain visible against different backgrounds.

Should I use a center dot crosshair?

A center dot provides a minimal aiming reference, but some players prefer traditional crosshair lines. Try both styles and choose the one that feels most comfortable.

Can a crosshair improve my aim?

A comfortable and visible crosshair can help with visual focus. However, aiming also depends on practice, positioning, crosshair placement and mouse control.
