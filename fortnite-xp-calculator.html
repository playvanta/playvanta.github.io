How to Calculate Fortnite XP

Enter your current level, target level, XP progress and expected XP per match. The calculator estimates your remaining XP and the number of matches required.

Fortnite progression rules and XP rewards may change between seasons or modes, so the result is a planning estimate rather than a live account calculation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much XP do I need to level up?

It depends on the current Fortnite progression system. You can adjust the assumed XP per level in this calculator.

Does the calculator connect to my Fortnite account?

No. It uses the numbers you enter and does not access your Epic Games account.
