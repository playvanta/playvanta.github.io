Conversions use commonly documented mouse-yaw values and assume comparable raw mouse input. Different FOV, ADS settings, zoom multipliers and game behavior can affect how aiming feels. eDPI is game-specific.

What Is eDPI?

eDPI means effective dots per inch. Multiply your mouse DPI by your in-game sensitivity to compare settings within the same game. For cross-game comparisons, cm/360° is often more meaningful.
