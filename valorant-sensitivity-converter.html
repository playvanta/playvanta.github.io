How to Convert Valorant Sensitivity

Select your original game, choose the destination game and enter your current sensitivity. The converter automatically calculates a new sensitivity using the games' approximate mouse yaw settings.

For example, converting a Valorant sensitivity of 0.35 to Counter-Strike 2 gives approximately 1.1136 at the same DPI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does DPI affect sensitivity conversion?

DPI affects physical mouse movement, but when you keep the same DPI between games, the conversion ratio is based on the games' yaw settings.

Will the converted sensitivity feel exactly the same?

Not necessarily. It aims to preserve 360-degree turning distance, but field of view and aiming mechanics can change the perceived sensitivity.
