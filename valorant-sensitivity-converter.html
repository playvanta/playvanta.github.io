🎯 FPS Sensitivity Converter

Convert mouse sensitivity between Valorant, Counter-Strike 2, Apex Legends and Overwatch 2 while keeping approximately the same physical mouse distance for a 360° turn.

Assumes default yaw settings: Valorant 0.07°, CS2/Apex 0.022°, Overwatch 2 0.0066° per sensitivity unit per mouse count. Custom yaw settings, scoped sensitivity, FOV, acceleration and game updates can change the feel. Test the result in-game.

How it works

For the same mouse DPI and 360° turn distance, multiply your original sensitivity by the source game's yaw coefficient and divide by the destination game's yaw coefficient.

Example: Valorant 0.35 × 0.07 ÷ 0.022 ≈ CS2 1.1136.
