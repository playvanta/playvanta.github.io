How to Use This Converter

Choose your current game and the game you want to convert to. Enter your in-game sensitivity and mouse DPI, then press Convert Sensitivity.

What Is eDPI?

eDPI means effective DPI. It is calculated by multiplying mouse DPI by in-game sensitivity. It is most useful for comparing settings within the same game, because games use different sensitivity scales.

What Is cm/360?

cm/360 is the physical mouse movement needed to turn your character 360 degrees. Matching cm/360 helps maintain a similar turning distance between games.

Conversions assume default mouse yaw settings, unchanged DPI and no mouse acceleration. Different field-of-view and aiming systems can still make games feel different.
