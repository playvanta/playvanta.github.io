Sensitivity Guide

How Does Valorant Sensitivity Conversion Work?

Different FPS games use different sensitivity scales. A numerical sensitivity value of 1.0 does not necessarily produce the same mouse movement in two different games.

One way to compare settings is to use the amount of physical mouse movement required to rotate your view by 360 degrees. This measurement is commonly called cm/360°.

What Is Mouse Yaw?

Mouse yaw describes how much your view rotates horizontally for a unit of mouse input at a given in-game sensitivity. The converter uses the following commonly documented default values.

Mouse-Yaw Values Used

Valorant: 0.07
Counter-Strike 2: 0.022
Apex Legends: 0.022
Overwatch 2: 0.0066

How to Calculate Equivalent Sensitivity

The converter uses the source sensitivity and the ratio between the two games' mouse-yaw values.

Target Sensitivity = Source Sensitivity × (Source Yaw ÷ Target Yaw)

This preserves the estimated physical mouse distance needed for a full turn when using the same DPI.

How to Calculate cm/360°

cm/360° = 914.4 ÷ (DPI × Sensitivity × Mouse Yaw)

A smaller cm/360° value means less physical mouse movement is needed to turn around. A larger value means more movement is required.

Aim Fundamentals

What Is eDPI in Valorant?

eDPI stands for effective dots per inch. It is calculated by multiplying your mouse DPI by your in-game sensitivity.

eDPI = Mouse DPI × In-Game Sensitivity
Example: 800 DPI and 0.35 Valorant Sensitivity

800 × 0.35 = 280 eDPI.

A player using 1600 DPI and 0.175 Valorant sensitivity would also have 280 eDPI. Under comparable input settings, both configurations have the same nominal turning sensitivity in Valorant.

eDPI is most meaningful when comparing players within the same game. Because games use different yaw values, identical eDPI numbers do not necessarily mean identical physical sensitivity across different games.

Frequently Asked Questions

Valorant Sensitivity Converter FAQ

How do I convert Valorant sensitivity to CS2?

Multiply your Valorant sensitivity by approximately 3.1818 using the default yaw values shown above. For example, 0.35 Valorant sensitivity converts to approximately 1.1136 in CS2.

Is Valorant sensitivity the same as Apex Legends?

Not numerically. Using the default yaw values, Apex Legends and CS2 share the same conversion factor from Valorant. Different game mechanics and FOV can still make aiming feel different.

What is a good eDPI for Valorant?

There is no universally ideal eDPI. Your preferred setting depends on mouse control, available desk space, playstyle and personal comfort. Test different settings rather than assuming one value is best for everyone.

What does cm/360 mean?

cm/360° measures the physical distance, in centimeters, that you move your mouse to rotate your view through one full 360-degree turn.

Does changing DPI affect sensitivity?

Yes. If you increase your mouse DPI without reducing in-game sensitivity, your effective turning sensitivity will increase under comparable input settings.

Does the converter match ADS or scoped sensitivity?

No. This calculator estimates default hip-fire horizontal turning sensitivity. ADS and scoped sensitivity can involve additional multipliers and visual differences.
